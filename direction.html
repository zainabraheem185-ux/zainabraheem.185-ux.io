My direction

Right now, I can picture myself becoming a software engineer. I'm especially interested in backend programming because I enjoy the logic and problem-solving behind how things work. I like the idea of building websites and applications that people can actually use. I'm also interested in data science because it involves finding patterns in data and using them to understand problems and make decisions. I want to explore both fields during my time at HZ and see which one suits my interests and strengths best.

More than anything, I want a career that keeps me curious and gives me room to grow. I hope to look back one day and feel proud of how far I've come, the things I've created, and the person I've become along the way.
