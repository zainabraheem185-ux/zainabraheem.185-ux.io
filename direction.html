My direction

At the moment, I see myself becoming a software engineer, especially in backend development. Software engineers design, build, test, and maintain applications. Backend developers focus on the logic and systems that make applications work behind the scenes, such as processing information and communicating with databases. I find this interesting because I enjoy problem-solving and want to understand how things work.

I am also interested in Data Science. Data scientists use programming, statistics, and data analysis to find patterns in information and help people make decisions. Although both fields require logical thinking and technical skills, Software Engineering focuses more on building and maintaining software, while Data Science focuses more on understanding and interpreting data.

At this stage, Software Engineering is my first choice because I like the idea of creating applications that people can actually use. However, I want to learn more about both fields during my time at HZ before making a final decision. I hope to gain practical experience through assignments, projects, and eventually an internship so I can understand which career best matches my interests and strengths.

More than anything, I want a career that keeps me curious and gives me room to grow. I hope to look back one day and feel proud of how far I've come, the things I've created, and the person I've become along the way.
