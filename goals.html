My Goals

During my ICT studies, I have several goals that I want to achieve both personally and professionally.

SWOT Description
Strengths One of my strengths is problem-solving. I enjoy figuring out how things work and trying different approaches when I face a challenge. This can help me with programming tasks and practical ICT projects.
Weaknesses Sometimes I lack confidence in my own work, especially when I do not understand something straight away. I can also lose interest when tasks become repetitive. I want to work on this by being more patient, breaking tasks into smaller steps, and recognizing my progress.
Opportunities HZ gives me opportunities to improve my programming skills through practical assignments, projects, and feedback from teachers. In the future, I can also look for internships to gain experience and learn how ICT is used in the workplace.
Threats Difficult programming concepts, tight deadlines, and assignments that take longer than expected could affect my progress. To manage these challenges, I will plan my time, practise regularly, and ask for help before I fall too far behind.

My Goals

Goal 1: Make the Most of My Strengths

I have always liked figuring things out and finding ways to get things done efficiently. I want to use this strength during my ICT studies by trusting my own ideas more and being willing to take on challenges without doubting myself too much. I hope to become someone who can turn an idea into something real.

Goal 2: Be a Little Kinder to Myself

Sometimes, when something gets too repetitive or difficult, I lose interest or start doubting myself. I want to learn to be more patient with myself and understand that not everything comes easily the first time. My goal is to keep trying, even when things get frustrating, and remind myself that getting better takes time.I want to practise regularly, break difficult problems into smaller steps, and ask for help when needed.

My Study Success Plan

To succeed in my ICT studies, I want to stay organized and keep up with my assignments instead of leaving everything until the last minute. I will practise programming regularly, break difficult problems into smaller steps, and ask for help when I get stuck. I also want to review feedback from assignments so I can understand my mistakes and improve next time. By keeping track of deadlines and making steady progress, I hope to pass my courses and gradually become a more confident programmer.
