ICT work

Software engineers do more than just write code. They help plan, build, test, and maintain software, and they need to solve problems when something goes wrong. Data scientists work more with data, using programming, statistics, and analysis to find patterns and answer questions. Both careers require logical thinking, patience, and a willingness to keep learning. After learning more about these fields, I can see why they interest me. I especially like the idea of creating something useful as a software engineer, but I'm also curious to discover more about working with data.

One thing I like about ICT is that there is always something new to discover. I don't want to stop learning once I graduate; I'd love to keep exploring new technologies, try different things, and find my own place in a field that is constantly changing.
