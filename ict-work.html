ICT work

Software engineers do more than just write code. They design, build, test, and maintain software, such as websites and applications. They also find and fix bugs to make sure their software works correctly. Data scientists work with data using programming, statistics, and analysis to find patterns and answer questions. Their work can help organizations understand problems and make decisions. Both careers require logical thinking, problem-solving, and a willingness to keep learning, but their main tasks are different. Software engineers focus on creating and maintaining software, while data scientists focus on analyzing data and interpreting results.
